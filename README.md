# BLOCKFARM 🌾

> **Hold to earn fee sharing on Robinhood Chain (4663)**  

Hold BLOCKFARM and every trade pays you — 100% of creator fees shared to holders, pro-rata, automatically. No claim needed.

---

## How it works

1. **HOLD** — buy & hold BLOCKFARM. Your share = balance ÷ total supply.
2. **TRADES PAY FEES** — every buy/sell pays a fee, creator portion earmarked for holders.
3. **EARN AUTOMATICALLY** — fees pushed to your wallet as BLOCKFARM + WETH, permanent by launchpad.

## Stack

- React + Vite
- GitHub Pages (relative base, works on subpath or custom domain)
- Real on-chain reads via Robinhood Chain RPC (balance, total supply)
- No fake data, no fake prices
